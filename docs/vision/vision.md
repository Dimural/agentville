# Vision

Purpose: why Agentville exists, who it's for, and the feeling it must create.

## One line

**A cozy pixel town that shows what your Claude Code sessions are doing, and tells you when one needs you.**

## Two jobs, equally important

1. **Useful.** Developers run several Claude Code sessions at once, in terminals, IDEs and the desktop app. The question that matters is *"which one just finished, and which one is stuck waiting for me?"* A character walking onto your screen answers it without you going to look.
2. **Delightful.** Pressing **Release the crew** and watching a dozen tiny characters pour out of a window, wander your desktop and hammer away at little terminals is the screenshot people share. Visual quality and charm are core features. They are not polish to add later.

## Who it's for

Developers on macOS who use Claude Code, often with several sessions at once. They care about focus, privacy and a fast machine, and they enjoy a bit of whimsy that stays out of the way.

## Personality: what "playful" means here

Playful is a design requirement, not decoration. Every one of these should be true:

- **Characters feel alive.** They blink, wander, meet each other ("hi!", "high five!"), nap with floating Z's, sip coffee, celebrate with confetti, get dizzy after a hard throw.
- **Small surprises reward attention.** A tap gives a "boop" and a happy hop. Being released prompts a "Wheee!" or "Freedom!". Stepping out of the crowd earns a "My turn!".
- **Physical and toy-like.** Squash and stretch, arcs, dust puffs on landing, bounces off screen edges. You can pick someone up and throw them.
- **Your crew is *yours*.** Each project folder always gets the same character, so you learn who's who. `api-server` is always the pink-shirted one in shades.
- **Never annoying.** Delight that interrupts work isn't delight. Charm lives at the edges: bottom-right walk-ons, capped counts, click-through always.

## What it is not (non-goals)

- Not a session manager, terminal, or dashboard of prompts and outputs.
- It never reads or shows what Claude writes.
- It doesn't control Claude Code: it can't approve, deny, stop or start anything.
- It doesn't run on Windows or Linux.
- It doesn't show cloud or remote sessions (claude.ai/code, SSH, containers).
- It isn't official. No Anthropic or Claude logos, mascots or brand marks. All art is original.

## Principles, in priority order

1. **Never harm the user's work.** Claude Code behaves identically with Agentville installed, running, quit or deleted. Clicks always reach what's underneath.
2. **Private by construction.** The app can't leak what it never receives.
3. **Light.** It's a toy that lives next to your real tools; it must cost almost nothing.
4. **Faithful to the prototype's charm.** When in doubt about look or motion, match the prototype.
5. **Simple to install, simple to remove.** One command in, one command out, nothing left behind.
